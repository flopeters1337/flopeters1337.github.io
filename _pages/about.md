# Background
I grew up in Belgium, the land of beer, chocolate and fries and in 2018, I graduated from the University of Liège with a Master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering with a strong focus on machine learning, optimization and artificial intelligence.

I then took on a researcher and teaching assistant position at HEC Liège Management School where I taught courses at the Bachelor's and Master's level. I managed a team of developers to deliver virtual and augmented reality projects within the [SIG](http://www.sig.hec.uliege.be/). I also conducted scientific research on flight delay predictions and the three-dimensional bin packing problem using machine learning and deep reinforcement learning techniques.

Afterwards, I worked as an operations researcher within [QuantOM](http://www.quantom.hec.ulg.ac.be/) on the City Line project. The objective of the project was to develop a digital platform for multi-modal collaborative freight transport. My contribution consisted in a robust software architecture for the decision-making module of the platform as well as novel optimization algorithms for the bi-objective problem of minimizing delivery costs and environmental impact in a collaborative transportation network. For more information, here is a [presentation of the project](https://youtu.be/tsf6fx6nB3M) (in French) done for LIEGE CREATIVE.

As of 2022, I've been working as Senior Software Engineer at Black Shamrock, a game development studio in Dublin, Ireland where I help create systems, tools, gameplay, and engine features in AA and AAA games.
