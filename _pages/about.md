Hello! I am a software engineer conducting research at [HEC Liège Management School](https://hec.uliege.be). My fields of interests are intelligent systems, machine learning, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, optimization and robotics. I am fluent in Python but can also speak C, C++, Java, PHP and some more exotic languages such as Scheme and Haskell.

My purpose is to make the world a more sustainable place with the help of automation and AI.

# Background
I grew up in Belgium, the land of beer, chocolate and fries.

In 2018, I graduated from the University of Liège with a Master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering. My curriculum had a strong focus on machine learning, optimization and artificial intelligence.

I then took on a researcher and teaching assistant position at HEC Liège Management School where I taught courses at the Bachelor's and Master's level. I helped develop virtual and augmented reality projects within the [SIG](http://www.sig.hec.uliege.be/). I also conducted research on flight delay predictions and the three-dimensional bin packing problem.

Currently I am working as researcher within [QuantOM](http://www.quantom.hec.ulg.ac.be/) on the City Line project. The objective is to develop a digital platform for multi-modal collaborative freight transport. My contribution consists in a robust software architecture for the decision matrix module as well as novel optimization algorithms for the bi-objective problem of minimizing delivery costs and environmental impact in a collaborative transportation network. For more information about this project, here is a [presentation of the project](https://youtu.be/tsf6fx6nB3M) (in French) done for LIEGE CREATIVE. 

## Notable Achivements
